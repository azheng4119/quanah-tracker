Star Force Permanent

When you die, your Inspector Level and Battlesim progress reset, and the score from those round missions is lost. Only boss and Star Force missions are kept, plus any Dimensional Skills you chose with Skill Inheritance and your Mapae Module levels. “Log a death” resets the Inspector Level and floor to 0 and clears every skill not marked to keep, so the tracker matches your new run. Values come from the Nov 11, 2026 Quanah event notes. Your entries are saved in this browser only.

Dimensional Skills

You can hold up to 5: pick from the 37 Active skills or the Passive Inspector Acumen. Getting a duplicate raises that skill’s level. Tick “Keep on death” for skills you picked with Skill Inheritance; you earn a pick at Inspector Lv. 50, 80 and 100.

Mapae Module Permanent

Points you put into a module stay after you die. You earn 1 point for each Dimensional Mission you complete and spend them with SEN-D in the Emergency Escape Room; unspent points are lost when you leave the room.
